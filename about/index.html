About Nuntii Batavi

Nuntii Batavi is the Dutch division of Nuntii Europae. It follows the news, the politics and the public mood of the Netherlands, and its relations with Brussels, calmly and without taking sides.

It writes in English, for Dutch readers at home with English, for internationals in the Netherlands and for people elsewhere in Europe who watch The Hague. The public mood comes from named polls, never from a guess.

Articles here are drafted with AI from published sources. An editor checks most of them before they appear; an article that went out unchecked says so. Illustrations are generated with AI.
